Digit reader test

Setup tips: glare, angle, alert 572°F, the box
  • Avoid glare. Tilt the phone or the thermometer until no reflection crosses the digits.
  • Face the display square-on. This LCD shows a faint double image at an angle.
  • Set the thermometer's alert temperature to 572°F (its maximum), so it never beeps or flashes its backlight mid-roast.
  • The box: drag it tight around the top-right number, the one under THERMO TEMP on the probe 1 row. Leave out the °F sign and the SET TEMP number. The dashed area to its left is read too, so a third digit can appear (99 to 100) even if the box was drawn around two digits.
